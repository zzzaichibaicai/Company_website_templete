🏢 About Us

Dongguan Changsheng Precision Machinery Co., Ltd. — Specializing in the trade of used imported injection molding machines

Dongguan Changsheng Precision Machinery Co., Ltd. is located in Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, and is a company specializing in the trade of used imported injection molding machines. We have been deeply involved in the injection molding machine industry for many years, accumulating extensive industry experience and a broad procurement network. We maintain a long-term stock of high-quality used injection molding machines from Japan, Germany, South Korea, and other countries.

Our main brands include internationally renowned names such as FANUC, SUMITOMO, JSW, NISSEI, TOSHIBA, TOYO, SODICK, ARBURG, ENGEL, and Woojin (LS). We also offer a complete range of auxiliary equipment for injection molding, including 3-in-1 integrated systems, mold temperature controllers, granulators, robots, chillers, and more, providing customers with one-stop procurement services.
